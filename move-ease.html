📌 Description

Move-Ease is a comprehensive moving and logistics management system with AI-powered assistance for seamless relocation services. The platform connects customers with truck owners and manages the entire moving process.

Features an intelligent AI agent powered by LangGraph and RAG to assist users with booking inquiries, policy questions, and service information. It streamlines booking management, payment processing, real-time tracking, and staff coordination.

✨ Key Features

Customer Portal

  • • Book moving services with multiple truck types (Mazda, Shehzore, Truck)
  • • Real-time truck availability checking
  • • Secure online payment via Stripe
  • • Booking tracking and management
  • • Service reviews and ratings
  • • Email notifications for booking updates

Truck Owner Portal

  • • Register and manage trucks
  • • View booking assignments
  • • Track earnings and payment history

Admin Dashboard

  • • Approve/reject bookings and truck registrations
  • • Assign trucks and staff to bookings
  • • Manage pricing and truck availability
  • • View analytics and reports
  • • Handle cancellations and refunds

Staff Management

  • • Staff assignment to bookings
  • • Status tracking (Active, Inactive, On Leave)
  • • Performance reviews

AI-Powered Assistant

  • • Natural language query handling using LangGraph
  • • RAG-based knowledge retrieval from company policies
  • • Voice interaction support via Cartesia
  • • Multi-language support with Groq LLM

Real-time Features

  • • Live booking tracking
  • • Automated email notifications
  • • Dynamic pricing calculation based on distance
