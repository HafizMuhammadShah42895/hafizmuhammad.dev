📌 What is CallerAI?

CallerAI is a custom-built AI-powered cold calling platform for businesses that want to automate outreach without giving up privacy or brand identity. It provides an always-available intelligent AI voice agent that holds natural two-way conversations with real customers, answers product questions, handles objections, detects farewells, and logs everything.

Web-Based Call

Share a unique secure link with the customer. They open it in their browser and speak directly to the AI. No phone line or app needed.

Direct Phone Call

The system dials the customer's actual phone number and starts a live AI-powered conversation via Telnyx PSTN integration.

✨ All Platform Features

User Authentication & Data Isolation

  • • Each business gets its own account; all data completely isolated per user
  • • No cross-account data access is possible under any circumstance

Company Profile & AI Knowledge Base

  • • Business fills in: industry, products, pricing, target market, USPs, and FAQs
  • • All data automatically embedded into a private ChromaDB vector knowledge base
  • • The AI uses this knowledge base during every live call via RAG retrieval

AI Agent Configuration

  • • Customize agent name (e.g. Alex, Zara, Sara)
  • • Choose call tone: Professional, Friendly, Assertive, or Consultative
  • • Select language: English, Urdu, or Arabic

Built-in CRM — Customer & Lead Management

  • • Add customers with name, phone, email, company, notes
  • • Write a custom campaign pitch per individual customer
  • • Track lead status: New → Contacted → Interested → Closed
  • • Filter, search, and view full contact history

Real-Time AI Voice Pipeline

  • • STT: Whisper Large v3 Turbo (Groq) for speech-to-text
  • • LLM: LLaMA 3.1 (Groq) streamed token-by-token
  • • TTS: Cartesia Sonic-2 streamed in real time — sub-second latency

Conversation Memory & Scope Guard

  • • Tracks products discussed, prices quoted, quantities across turns — no contradictions
  • • Scope Guard: AI strictly stays on business topics, politely deflects unrelated questions
  • • Interruption detection: AI stops and listens when customer starts speaking
  • • Farewell detection: polite closing when customer signals disinterest or goodbye

AI-Powered Call Analysis & Insights Dashboard

  • • Auto-generated plain-English call summary (2–3 sentences) after each call
  • • Sentiment rating: Positive, Neutral, or Negative
  • • Outcome label: Interested, Not Interested, Requested Follow-up, or Hung Up Early
  • • Dashboard shows all call logs paginated and sorted by most recent
